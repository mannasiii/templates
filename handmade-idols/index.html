The Artist Behind the Art

Art is not just a creation, it's an expression.

Meet the Artist – Gurudas Sadanand Khandeparkar

With over 30 years of experience in the world of art, I am deeply committed to preserving and promoting traditional, spiritual, and contemporary artistic expressions. Holding a G.D. Art (Painting) degree, I have dedicated my life to mentoring students, reviving cultural art forms, and exploring the spiritual aspects of creativity.

As the founder of Ishakala Studio, my mission is to inspire and educate art lovers about the profound impact of art on the mind, soul, and energy field. My work extends beyond conventional art, focusing on the spiritual essence of creation, particularly through Sattvik Ganesha idol-making, Mandala art, and divine compositions. Through Ishakala Studio, I strive to create a space where art becomes a medium of transformation and inner harmony. Each creation is infused with devotion and intention, reflecting the deep connection between artistic expression and spiritual energy.

Our Artistic World

Ishakala Studio

At Ishakala Studio, we offer a harmonious blend of creativity, spirituality, and artistic excellence through our diverse range of services. Our Online Art Classes allow learners of all levels to explore traditional, spiritual, and modern techniques from the comfort of their homes, guided by expert mentorship. We create Custom Paintings that capture emotions, memories, and divine themes, transforming ideas into soulful artworks. Our Handmade Idols are crafted with devotion using Sattvik materials, radiating purity and spiritual energy. Through Photo Restoration and Idol Restoration, we breathe new life into cherished photographs and sacred idols, preserving their essence with utmost care. For those who love digital creativity, our Digital Painting service combines tradition with technology to produce vibrant, meaningful artworks. We also offer Photoshop Basic Training, empowering learners to edit and enhance images with professional finesse. Adding a festive touch, our Rangoli Stickers and Stencils bring color, culture, and creativity into every celebration. Each service at Ishakala Studio reflects our mission to inspire, educate, and connect people with the deeper essence of art — where every creation becomes a journey of devotion, transformation, and inner harmony.

Every creation begins with intention, devotion, and a deep understanding of energy and balance. We believe that colors, forms, and symbols hold the power to heal, inspire, and elevate consciousness. Whether it is the gentle strokes of a brush, the sacred shaping of a Ganesha idol, or the intricate detailing of a digital artwork, each process is infused with mindfulness and purity.
